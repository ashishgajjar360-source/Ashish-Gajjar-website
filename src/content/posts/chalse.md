"ચાલશે" (Chalse) is more than just a Gujarati word — it reflects a familiar mindset. It's the phrase people casually use when something is "good enough," often carrying a sense of comfort, laziness, or postponement. This artwork translates that everyday expression into typography by embedding meaning directly into its letterforms.
